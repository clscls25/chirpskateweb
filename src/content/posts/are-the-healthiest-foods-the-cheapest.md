For the healthiest version of yourself it may not be selecting healthy foods but rather excluding the unhealthiest ones. There's no doubt unhealthy foods are inexpensive and they also taste good and can have heavy marketing around them but what about healthy foods? Apples, oatmeal, carrots, blueberries, vegetables, potatoes, beans, whole grains, all don't give an idea of being expensive.
